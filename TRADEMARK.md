# Trademark and branding policy

The **code** of Internet Xtreme Downloader is free software under the
[MIT licence](LICENSE). You may use it, study it, change it and redistribute
it, including commercially. That does not change.

The **name and the branding** are a separate thing, and this page covers them.
A copyright licence such as MIT grants rights to the code; it grants no rights
to a project's name or its logo, and this document says plainly what is and is
not allowed so nobody has to guess.

## The marks

* **Internet Xtreme Downloader**
* **IXD**
* The application icon and logo, and the visual design of the website and the
  application interface.

They belong to the project's author.

## What you may do without asking

* **Refer to the project by name**, truthfully. "Works with Internet Xtreme
  Downloader", "a fork of IXD", "compatible with IXD" — all fine.
* **Redistribute the official builds unchanged**, under the official name, with
  the original release notes and this policy included. Mirrors, package
  repositories and download sites are welcome to do this.
* **Write about it, review it, teach it, screenshot it**, and link to the
  releases.
* **Fork the code and build your own version** — that is what the MIT licence
  is for. See the next section for what to call it.

## What needs written permission

* **Calling a modified version "Internet Xtreme Downloader" or "IXD"**, or
  anything close enough to be mistaken for it. Fork it under a different name.
  Your users should be able to tell whose build they are running, and if a fork
  breaks something the support requests should not arrive here.
* **Using the icon or logo as the icon of another application**, or as your own
  product's branding.
* **Domain names, app-store listings, social accounts or repositories** whose
  name suggests they are the official project.
* **Selling, bundling or advertising something under these marks**, or in any
  way that implies this project endorses, sponsors or supports it.

## The one promise this project makes about price

**Internet Xtreme Downloader is free, and it will stay free.** No ads, no
account, no paid tier, no "pro" version, nothing held back. That is a promise
about *this* project under *this* name — which is precisely why the name is
protected: if a paid build calling itself IXD appears, the promise is worth
nothing.

## Asking

Open an issue at <https://github.com/arminmacx/IXD/issues> and say what you want
to do. Permission is given in writing or not at all, and silence is not
permission.

## Third-party components

The application bundles **Qt**, through PySide6, under the **LGPL v3**. That
licence carries obligations of its own for anyone distributing a build, and
they are not affected by anything on this page.
