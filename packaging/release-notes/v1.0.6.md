## Fixed

- **A schedule could never be added.** Choosing the actions in the dialog
  produced a value the database refused, the error died inside the dialog, and
  the schedule silently never appeared — which also left "Downloads and order…"
  saying "Select a schedule first" for ever. Queues had the same fault.
- **Instagram offered the sound and not the picture.** Instagram publishes a
  clip as two `.mp4` files, one with no sound and one with no picture, fetched
  in slices. Every slice was recorded as a separate download of its own, the
  sound-only half was taken for a video, and the sound was small enough to be
  discarded as an interface beep. A clip is now one entry, the two halves are
  recognised for what they are, and picking the video downloads it *with* its
  own sound.

## Added

- **The Log says when a page is protected.** A site that plays under DRM —
  Spotify and the like — now says so in the Log, instead of leaving you
  wondering why nothing was detected. Encrypted streams cannot be downloaded,
  and the honest answer is better than silence.
