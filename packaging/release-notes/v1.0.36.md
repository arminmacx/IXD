## Added

- **Twitch recordings download at the quality you pick.** Past broadcasts,
  highlights and uploads now offer the full list — source, 720p60, 480p, 360p,
  160p and audio-only — each with its resolution and size, and the file is named
  after the video instead of a line of gibberish from the address. Before this,
  a Twitch page reported "no embedded media found", and a stream handed over by
  the browser had one nameless quality that was 360p whatever you asked for.

Live channels are unchanged: a live stream is still only downloadable as the
last few seconds it is currently serving.
