## Fixed

- **Streaming sites offered one download instead of a quality list.** A page
  publishing 1080p, 720p and 480p showed a single row for everything except
  YouTube and Vimeo. The panel now reads what the site published and lists
  every resolution it offers.
- **Cancel in a download's window now closes it**, instead of leaving the
  window open on a cancelled transfer.
- **The Log no longer repeats the taskbar's progress line.** It says once that
  the taskbar is working; after that only failures are recorded.

Update the extension along with the application: reload it from your browser's
extensions page after the first launch of this version.
