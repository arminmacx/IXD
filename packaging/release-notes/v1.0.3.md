## Changed

- **The video panel can be moved, and closed.** It now has a × in its corner,
  and dragging it puts it anywhere in the window — so it never has to sit on
  top of what you are watching. Closing it lasts until you reload the page; to
  switch it off everywhere, use the extension's options.

Update the extension along with the application: reload it from your browser's
extensions page after the first launch of this version.
