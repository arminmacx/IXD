## Fixed

- **"Downloads continue in the background" was said every time you closed the
  window.** Once is useful; after that you know. It is now shown on the first
  close only.
- **The notification announcing a download could show the wrong name.** On
  sites whose address ends in an identifier rather than a filename — GitHub's
  releases among them — the notification carried that identifier even though
  the window showed the real name. The origin is now asked before the
  notification is written, and only for addresses that need it.

## For anyone on 1.0.8

This is the first release your copy can update itself to: **Settings →
Updates → Check now**, or wait for the daily check. A `-selfupdate` build
downloads it, closes, replaces itself and starts again on its own.

Reload the extension in your browser afterwards.
